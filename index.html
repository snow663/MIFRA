MIFRA / RESEARCH PROTOTYPE

Mechanical Inference & Functional Reasoning Assessment

Twenty unfamiliar mechanical components. Identify what they do and explain how their geometry supports your reasoning.

Assessment preparation

This assessment is not yet open for submissions. Twenty component photographs must be independently verified, and a private response-collection service must be configured before participants can begin.

We will assess fundamental function (3 points), operating mechanism (2), application (2), design interpretation (2), and technical identification (1) for each exhibit. Technical names are optional.

Participants will be asked to respond without searching, indicate whether they recognize each component, and report their mechanical experience. Results will be scored by a human examiner.

Exhibits: 0 of 20 verified · Collection: not configured

About the study

The objective is to measure mechanical inference rather than recall alone. A valid comparison requires all participants to receive the same images, prompts, and scoring criteria.

Do not submit personal or sensitive information. Participant answers will not be collected on this page until a privacy notice and secure submission system are available.
